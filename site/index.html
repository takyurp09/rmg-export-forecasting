US revenue at risk

Each US product group moves by shock × group beta × pass-through. Betas come from past annual growth (2011-2025).

S2: EU duty after EBA ends

Duty-free EU access (EBA) runs for 3 years after LDC graduation, so a duty cannot apply before about Nov 2029. Without GSP+, 12% is the likely rate: standard GSP for apparel is suspended for a country above a 14.5% share, which Bangladesh would pass. The 9.6% standard-GSP rate is unlikely.

EU revenue at risk

Value change = (1 + duty)−elasticity − 1, applied to the EU knit and woven baselines.
